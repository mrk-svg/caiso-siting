KNOWN DEFECTS
-------------
This directory ships with a register of known errors in KNOWN_ISSUES.md at the
repository root. Read it before quoting any figure. In particular, a node's position,
county and utility are known to be wrong for a specific list of nodes, and the EIA-860
columns describe capacity within 5 km of a node rather than capacity interconnected at
that node.
